# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is an American *sci-fi horror drama* television series created by the Duffer Brothers. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, it follows a group of kids as they uncover government experiments and supernatural forces.

---

## Why I Love This Show
* **Nostalgic 80s Vibe:** Amazing soundtrack, fashion, and retro references.
* **Great Character Development:** Every character grows significantly each season.
* **Thrilling Plot:** A perfect mix of mystery, suspense, and friendship.

## Main Characters
1. **Eleven (Jane Hopper)** – A girl with psychokinetic abilities.
2. **Mike Wheeler** – The loyal leader of the friend group.
3. **Dustin Henderson** – The smart and humorous problem-solver.
4. **Jim Hopper** – The protective chief of police in Hawkins.

## Favorite Quote
> "Friends don't lie." — *Eleven*
